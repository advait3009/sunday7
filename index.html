Paediatric Dermatology

Expert skin care for infants, children and adolescents. Managing eczema, nappy rash, molluscum, viral warts and paediatric psoriasis with age-appropriate treatment.

02

Venereology & STI Management

Confidential, non-judgmental diagnosis and treatment of sexually transmitted infections. Discreet, compassionate care following national clinical guidelines.

Laser Skin Procedures

Q-Switch laser for pigmentation and tattoo removal. Fractional laser for scar resurfacing. Long-pulse laser for hair removal and vascular lesions.

Acne & Acne Scar Therapy

Comprehensive acne management: systemic antibiotics, isotretinoin protocols, chemical peels and micro-needling for scarring.

Diagnosis and treatment of onychomycosis, alopecia areata, androgenetic alopecia, scalp psoriasis and other nail & hair conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Venereology? +

Venereology is the branch of medicine dealing with sexually transmitted infections (STIs). Dr. Kshitij provides confidential, compassionate STI diagnosis and treatment.

Is the clinic safe for young children? +

Absolutely. Dr. Garg has 18 years of paediatric dermatology experience and a child-friendly consulting approach ensuring minimal stress for young patients.

How is vitiligo treated here? +

We use NBUVB phototherapy, topical immunomodulators and excimer laser for active vitiligo. Surgical melanocyte transplant is an option for stable vitiligo.

Is my medical information kept private? +

All records — especially STI-related — are stored with complete confidentiality following HIPAA-equivalent Indian privacy regulations. Zero disclosure without consent.
